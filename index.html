WHO WE ARE

About ESSA

The Electronics Engineering Students Association (ESSA) brings together students interested in electronics, communication, embedded systems, technology and innovation.

ESSA provides opportunities to learn beyond the classroom through technical events, workshops, seminars, competitions, project exhibitions and student activities.

Our goal is to encourage creativity, technical knowledge, teamwork and leadership while building a strong engineering community.
